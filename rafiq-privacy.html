Rafiq - Prayer, Quran & Qibla

Privacy Policy

How Rafiq handles information when you use the Android app.

Effective date: November 23, 2026

This policy describes Rafiq, an Android application developed by AtlasThought. It covers information handled in the app, on the device, and by the services used to provide account and content features.

Information you provide

  • Account information: If you sign in with Google, Supabase Auth processes your account identifier, email address, and profile information made available through the sign-in provider.
  • Profile and preferences: Information you choose to enter, such as display name, age, gender, reading level, preferred Adhan voice, city or region, prayer calculation choices, madhhab, theme, font size, and notification settings.
  • Saved and reading data: Quran reading position and progress, bookmarks, saved Quran verses, duas and hadith, and personal calendar events.

Location and device permissions

If you grant location permission, the app uses your device location to calculate prayer times and Qibla direction. Exact latitude and longitude are processed on your device and are not uploaded to your Rafiq account. You can instead enter or select a city manually. Compass sensor readings are used on the device to show direction.

Notifications are scheduled on your device. Notification permission is used for reminders you enable. Optional exact-alarm special access (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM) is requested for precisely timed prayer alerts; if it is off, Android may delay an alert. You can change this access in system settings. The app does not use your contacts to operate its core features.

Where information is stored

Account profile details, preferences, and saved items associated with your account are stored in Supabase Postgres and protected by access rules intended to let an authenticated user access only their own rows. Some app data, downloaded content, preferences, and reading state can also be stored locally on your device. If you uninstall or clear app storage, local-only information may be removed.

Content providers and credits

When online, Rafiq requests Quran text and streams or downloads Surah recitations through AlQuran.cloud (Islamic Network). These requests may include standard connection information such as your IP address; Rafiq does not send your profile or account data with Quran-content requests. The Arabic Quran text is credited to AlQuran.cloud, and the English translation is Sahih International. Recitations are used for personal and educational study under the provider terms; rights remain with their reciters and rights holders. The Adhan recording is bundled with Rafiq and works offline: “Beautiful adhan” by Adam-synagda, dedicated under CC0 1.0.

The English translation used by the app is Sahih International. Quran text and recordings are presented as supplied by their respective providers. Recitations remain subject to the rights and terms of the relevant reciter, publisher, or provider. See AlQuran.cloud's current terms and its credits page. The app's Quran and recitation credits are also available in the app's Privacy screen.

Why information is used

  • Sign you in and synchronize supported profile details, preferences, saved items, and reading data.
  • Provide prayer-time calculations, Qibla direction, Quran reading, saved content, reminders, and app settings.
  • Respond to support, account-deletion, security, and legal requests.

Sharing and service providers

Supabase provides authentication and cloud database services. Google provides the sign-in option. Quran and recitation providers deliver content when you use those features. These services process information under their own policies. AtlasThought does not sell your personal information.

Retention and account deletion

Cloud data is retained while your account is active, unless it is removed earlier or retention is required for a legitimate security or legal reason. You can delete your account in Rafiq from Profile → Settings → Delete my account. You may also submit an account-deletion request online. The online form records a request for review; it does not delete the account immediately. Deleting your account removes its associated cloud profile, preferences, and saved items. Local data stored on your device may need to be cleared separately by removing the app or clearing its storage.

Children's privacy

Rafiq is for users aged 13 and older. Profile setup and profile editing reject ages below 13. Some regions require a higher age to consent to online data processing; users must also meet any higher minimum age required where they live. If you believe an underage account has been created, contact Rafiq support to request review and deletion.

Security

We use Supabase authentication and database access controls to protect account data. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your device and sign-in account protected and contact us if you suspect unauthorized access.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app, its services, or applicable requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when a revision is made.

Contact

For Rafiq privacy questions or requests, email rafiqsupport@atlasthought.dev. Do not send passwords or authentication codes.
