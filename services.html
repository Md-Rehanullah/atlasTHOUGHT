Have an app idea?

We develop apps around unique concepts and timely ideas, and we take on app requests from others.

Request an app

Tell us what you want to build, who it is for, and which features matter most. We can discuss the scope and an estimate with you. Pricing depends on the app's genre, features, and agreed scope; we do not publish fixed rates.

To discuss a project, email atlasthoughtofficial@gmail.com.

What to include in a project inquiry

To help us understand your idea, describe the problem the app should solve, who it is for, the features that matter most, and any designs or technical requirements you already have. We can use those details to discuss a practical scope and estimate.

Collaborate with atlasTHOUGHT

We welcome people interested in working together on distinctive concepts and community-centered apps. Write to us about your skills, interests, or an idea you would like to explore.
